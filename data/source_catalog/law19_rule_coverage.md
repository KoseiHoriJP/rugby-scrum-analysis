## Annotation contract

- Use `unreviewed`, `satisfied`, `not_satisfied`, `not_observable`, or `not_applicable` for a rule assessment. Missing visibility is `not_observable`, never automatic failure or compliance.
- Retain parent introductions and the principle as informational context. Observe their commands and conditions without inventing an extra offence. Apply alternatives as alternatives and retain permitted choices.
- Store referee decisions and explanations independently. A safety whistle, reset, collapse or official teaching-video reference does not establish a penalty, intention, causal actor or guilty player.
- Keep observable action, causal attribution and `intent_hypothesis` separate. Leave player, team or role labels null when evidence cannot establish them; shirt number alone does not establish responsibility.
- `observation_fields` name types in a normalized observations table, with time, evidence and JSON values. They are not compulsory CSV columns. JSON includes the full evidence/context registry and literal clause text.

## Source qualifications retained

- **19.1:** restart cause, location and throwing entitlement require the 17 table rows and often preceding-play footage.
- **19.6:** record players on field and in each scrum, the minimum of five and the permission for the opposing team to keep different scrum numbers. Law 3.15 is referenced but not reproduced.
- **19.13–19.18:** either feed side may be chosen; diagram-specific ball hold is pending asset review; legal play starts on ball leaving hands (19.16), and gaining possession by driving opponents back is permitted (19.18).
- **19.25–19.26:** separate a safety whistle from fault. “Use it” follows stationary, available ball for three–five seconds and is not itself an end event.
- **19.28–19.30:** preserve alternative offside positions. Only **19.30(a)** is explicitly marked **GLOBAL LAW TRIAL**; do not extend that marker to (b), (c) or the entire law.
- **19.33–19.36:** scrum offside lines cease on scrum end. Reset conditions inherit “when there is no infringement”; 19.34(e) has a repeated-kick intentionality exception and printed penalty. The same team throws after reset (19.35).
- **19.37–19.38:** visible collapse, lift, fall or misleading movement is distinct from intention and responsible-player identity. Preserve the different clause-specific sanctions from the clause catalog.
- **19.39:** the union may adopt under-19 variations at defined levels; the exact variations are not supplied and remain outside this source’s assessed requirements.
